# Opening Night

Kinetic's first release through [Debut](https://github.com/andretmarques/debut), a new tool that signs, notarizes and publishes Mac apps with one command.

Nothing changes in the app itself, so your settings and permissions should carry over.

## Install

1. [Download `Kinetic.dmg`](https://github.com/andretmarques/Kinetic/releases/download/alpha-1.2/Kinetic.dmg), open it and drag **Kinetic** to **Applications**.
2. Open Kinetic, then turn on **Kinetic Agent** under **Accessibility** and **Input Monitoring** if asked.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Runs on Apple silicon and Intel.
