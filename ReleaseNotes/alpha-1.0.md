# Tamed Curve

The first public alpha of Kinetic: your PC mouse and keyboard, finally feeling native on the Mac.

- **A pointer you can trust.** Set speed and acceleration per mouse, including truly linear movement.
- **Scrolling your way.** Reverse the mouse wheel and leave your trackpad alone.
- **Any button, any action.** Give your side buttons a job: desktops, Mission Control, media, apps and shortcuts.
- **A Mac keyboard, from any keyboard.** Swap Alt and Win, and get Mac F-keys.
- **So light you'll forget it's there.** Around 30 MB of memory with the menu bar icon on, and about 5 MB with it off just after starting. 0% CPU when idle, no drivers.

This is an alpha, so expect rough edges and please report what you find.
